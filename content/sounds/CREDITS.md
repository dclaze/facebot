Source pages and licenses checked October 25, 2026. Original assets are retained in sources/; selection.json records URLs, hashes, excerpt boundaries and speed changes. source-receipts/ preserves source-page and pack-license evidence.

Facebot modifications: excerpt selection; stereo-to-mono downmix; 24 kHz resampling; 45 Hz high-pass rumble filtering; silence trimming with lower threshold to preserve decay; 2 ms attack/50 ms release fades; RMS level matching constrained to -9 dBFS peak headroom. Longer natural drum and trombone endings are preserved within a six-second limit. Variant speed/pitch changes are explicitly recorded in selection.json. No aggressive denoising is applied to clean sources.

Freesound assets downloaded as the public HQ MP3 preview are identified as such; they are not represented as lossless originals. The comic drum sting is a creator-performed sampled-kit recording. Boing is a recorded jaw harp; bell is a recorded desk bell; drumroll is a recorded snare; sad trombone is Benboncan’s performance. Kenney arcade/sci-fi sounds are creator-designed electronic effects, not field recordings.

No endorsement is implied. Retain attribution, license links and change descriptions when redistributing. Earlier recognition-screen.json results describe the retired revision only and do not establish listening quality for these replacements.
